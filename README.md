# Excel-Sales-Data-Analysis-Project

## Business Findings & Recommendations

### Key Findings

1. October achieved the highest monthly sales (₹3.14M), while September recorded the lowest sales (₹0.96M).
2. The West region was the top-performing region, generating ₹6.54M in sales and ₹624.15K in profit.
3. Higher discounts were associated with lower average profit margins. The margin decreased from 21.43% at 0% discount to 9.11% at 25% discount.

### Practical Recommendations

1. **Optimize Discount Strategy:** Review high-discount offers and introduce targeted promotions to protect profit margins.
2. **Focus on High-Performing Regions and Products:** Strengthen sales activities in the West region and promote popular products while improving the profitability of lower-performing products.
